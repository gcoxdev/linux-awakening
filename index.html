>_ Linux Awakening

Learn the Linux command line by living a story.

Every command you type happens in a real-feeling Linux machine, and every chapter teaches a little more. Pick a story — your progress in each is saved separately in this browser.

Your Linux

Most commands are the same on every distribution. The package manager, the admins' group and a few defaults follow the one you pick — choose the one you use, or want to learn.

Both stories cover the same ground — navigating, reading and editing files, pipes, permissions, processes, services, archives, scripting and networking. They're written differently: pick the one that sounds like more fun. The Practice Range has no story at all — just an endless supply of real tasks to sharpen what you've learned.
